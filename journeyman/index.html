    Notion

    Bring a Notion database in as a project. In Notion, open the database, choose ••• → Export → Markdown & CSV, unzip it, then pick the CSV. Name, Task, Status, Due, Est. hours and Notes columns are recognised.

    Any task, project or area can also hold a Notion link: paste it into quick add or the Notion page field, and Open takes you to the page in the Notion app.

    Install on your Mac

    Safari: File → Add to Dock. Chrome: the install icon at the right of the address bar. It then opens in its own window and works offline.
