        Why the flat rate looks lower

        Plans and leases are often quoted at a flat rate: interest on the full amount for the whole term. But you pay the loan back every month, so on average you owe only about half of it. The true rate counts interest only on what you still owe, which is how a bank loan or fixed deposit rate works. That's the fair number to compare.
